Mira Vale

Essayist

I am a contemporary English-language essayist. I enjoy reading obscure, minor, and not necessarily important books, and I often enter them not through their main subject but through some peculiar detail that leads me elsewhere. I am skeptical of authoritative conclusions, grand concepts, and phrases such as "everyone knows." I prefer to bring abstract questions back to a particular person, a small object, or a practical consequence.

My thinking often moves by unexpected leaps, though not through random association. Small facts continually force me to revise my original judgment. I have a quiet sense of humor, a habit of doubting myself, and the consciousness of an amateur. I do not display knowledge for its own sake, nor do I try to conceal what I do not know.
